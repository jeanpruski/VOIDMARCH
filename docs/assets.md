# Assets

Le nouvel accueil lovecraftien et son prompt sont documentés dans [assets-cosmic.md](assets-cosmic.md).

Le rendu des figurines passe désormais par `apps/web/src/sprite-atlas.ts` : les silhouettes sont isolées par leurs pixels transparents, attribuées à leur case principale puis ajustées proportionnellement dans des cellules de 256 pixels avec une marge de 20 pixels. Phaser et les vignettes HTML utilisent ces mêmes atlas préparés en mémoire. Les PNG originaux sont conservés. Les positions CSS sont proportionnelles à la taille des vignettes, y compris sur mobile.

Les nouveaux visuels de la direction « Cendres et acier », leurs chemins et leurs prompts sont documentés dans [assets-industrial.md](assets-industrial.md).

`apps/web/public/assets/expansion.png` : atlas de 24 nouvelles figurines (12 unités, 12 bâtiments), grille 6 × 4 de cellules 256 × 256. Généré avec imagegen en reprenant le style de l'atlas initial. Les deux essais de génération ont produit un damier opaque ; sur autorisation explicite de l'utilisateur, `scripts/remove-sprite-background.py` a retiré le fond neutre connecté aux bords et créé le canal alpha. Original conservé dans `.data/expansion-original.png`. Commande : `python3 scripts/remove-sprite-background.py .data/expansion-original.png apps/web/public/assets/expansion.png`.

Prompt exact de génération :

Input image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY; create a NEW sheet with all different subjects. Exactly 6 columns x 4 rows, canvas 1536x1024, each cell 256x256, one isolated object per cell, generous transparent padding. TRUE transparent alpha background, no colored background, no text, no lines, no hex platforms. Match the reference's exquisite hand-painted weathered medieval miniature style, consistent slightly overhead 3/4 view, desaturated moss green and old grey stone, iron, muted burgundy fabric, sparse warm firelight. Row 1 left to right: humble peasant in linen tunic carrying an axe and basket; ragged militia with small round shield and club; disciplined long spear soldier; armored crossbowman with visible crossbow; green hooded forest ranger with longbow; light cavalry scout on small horse with javelin. Row 2: heavily armored paladin on foot with pale tabard and hammer; wheeled wooden covered battering ram; hooded female healer carrying bandages and lantern; engineer with leather apron, large hammer and tool bag; bare-armed fur-cloaked berserker with two axes; hooded ancient acolyte carrying a stone relic with a very subtle cyan seam. Row 3: tiny pioneer camp with one canvas tent, campfire and crates; modest thatched medieval house; raised wooden granary with grain sacks; hunter hut with drying racks; small riverside fishing cabin with net and tied boat; wooden stable with two horse stalls and fenced yard. Row 4: archery training yard with straw targets; small weathered stone monastery and bell tower; forge with anvil, orange furnace and bellows; medieval stone library with arched windows and one tiny pale star emblem; medieval bakery with rounded stone bread oven and woodpile; old covered stone well with water barrels. Every silhouette distinct and readable at small size. Entire objects inside their cells; same ground-contact baseline around 85% of each cell. No modern guns, no neon, no sci-fi machinery, no cartoon look, no gore. 70% medieval, 20% cosmic mystery, 10% ancient incomprehensible technology.

Create a precisely aligned 6-column by 4-row grid, 1536x1024 image, each cell exactly 256x256. Transparent background throughout. No lines, no text, no frames, no hex bases, no cast shadows outside cells. Exactly one centered isolated miniature per cell with generous transparent margin. Consistent 3/4 slightly overhead isometric camera, exquisitely hand-painted detailed game miniature style, clear small silhouettes, weathered grey stone and steel, dark moss roofs, muted burnt red cloth, tiny warm windows. All objects entirely inside their own cell, similar visual scale, bottom-aligned at 85% of each cell. Row 1 left to right: cloaked scout with lantern and short spear; infantry swordsman with shield; broad heavy guard with huge shield; archer with obvious longbow; mounted knight with lance; wooden iron medieval trebuchet (not cannon). Row 2 left to right: wooden outpost tower with tent; small medieval village of three houses; dense walled borough; monumental compact fortified city; farm with geometric golden fields; lumbermill with stacked logs. Row 3 left to right: rocky iron mine entrance; medieval market with cloth tents; timber stone warehouse; blacksmith workshop; fortified barracks courtyard; massive compact stone keep. Row 4 left to right: tall narrow medieval watchtower; ancient seamless black monolith with one fine cyan seam; dark vitrified glass crater; hollow medieval knight armor with a faint stellar light inside; medieval covered trade caravan wagon; impossible ancient stone portal arch. Art direction 70% dark medieval, 20% cosmic dread, 10% alien technology; ONLY the monolith hollow knight and portal have extremely subtle spectral cyan accents. Clear readable game assets, beautiful miniature illustration, dark outlines. No modern weapons, no holograms, no clean science fiction, no gore. Keep strict equal cell spacing and transparent padding.
